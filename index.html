About

We bring together students, researchers and practitioners to explore how technology is reshaping finance. We examine the technological, economic, entrepreneurial, legal and policy dimensions of financial innovation, connect research with practice, and create space for ideas, collaboration and ventures.

Recent Event

CV Summit 2026

29–30 September 2026 · Kongresshaus Zurich

As a Community Partner of CV Summit 2026, FinTech Lab St. Gallen brought a strong group of HSG students to Zurich for two days of exchange with founders, investors, financial institutions and technology companies.

Our community explored developments across digital assets, AI, blockchain and financial infrastructure, while building new connections across the Swiss and international FinTech ecosystem.
